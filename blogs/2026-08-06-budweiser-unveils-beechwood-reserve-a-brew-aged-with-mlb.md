To celebrate the 2026 MLB at Field of Dreams Game, Budweiser is introducing Budweiser Beechwood Reserve, a limited-edition brew aged with MLB-authenticated Marucci baseball bats crafted from the same beechwood used in Budweiser's signature aging process.
